# Refrains, Rihanna, and Rodrigo: Learning Place in Backstage Theater Space

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
In a black box theater on a university campus, a duel for control over a contested territory erupts between actors and their stage manager. The territory in question? The dressing room. The key combatants? Rihanna and Olivia Rodrigo. Through micro-analysis of a pre-show rehearsal, this study explores how theatre artists learn the malleability of space and place through negotiating territories, paying particular attention to the interactional entangling of affect, bodies, and sound in defining and mediating which spaces and places are enacted.

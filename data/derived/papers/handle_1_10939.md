# Achievify’s Learning-Centric and AI-powered Guide to Charting Career Paths

**Conference:** ISLS 2024

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
We introduced Achievify, a platform for AI-powered career guidance designed specifically for underserved communities. It emphasizes inclusivity and removes obstacles to accessibility by utilizing machine learning and natural language processing to provide simple navigation and career prediction. In addition to providing a dynamic employment information database, Achievify promotes educational advancement by introducing users to a range of career options, improving digital literacy, and encouraging lifelong learning.

## Problem statement

Major obstacles stand in the way of underserved com munities, particularly minorities and low-income communities, getting access to career possibilities  and information. Cultural barriers, a lack of awareness about leveraging skills, and difficulty accessing key job  information are challenges they face  (Johnson et al., 2007; Thiem & Dasgupta, 2022). Traditional higher education leaves a gap in knowle dge about possible job choices since it is typically out of reach for many (Chan, 2016). Unreliable material on the internet and complex webs ite layouts further restrict job research, and the COVID-19 pandemic exacerbated all of these issues and disparities. Solution, technology, and current progress Our inquiry has led to an innovative solution: an A I-powered career guidance system designed for underse rved communities. Our methodology adheres to four key principles: Data-Informed Decisions, Subject Expertise, Community Voices, and Needs-Based Iterations. We deliver customized career info rmation, identify critical timing for decision-making, and offer strategies for career advancement. Utilizing advanced machine learning and natural language processing techniques, our system is built for ease of use across both mobile and web-based platforms. With a focus on inclusion and accessibility, it considers individual interests and circumstanc es to present viable career options and outline key miles tones. Notably, the tool's impartial approach broad ens the spectrum of career possibilities to include not onl y traditional occupations but also tho se that do not necessitate formal training. Achievify elevates the user experience through a ca refully selected technology stack. We utilize Next.js for front-end development, ensuring high performance and sear ch engine optimization to enhance discoverability and user interaction. The backend i s powered by Google Firebase, which efficiently man ages database operations, data storage, and user authent ication. At the heart of o ur application is an AI-driven user interface, leveraging OpenAI's GPT-3.5-Turbo and GPT-4 models for natural language processing. These cuttingedge technologies empower Achievify to provide user s with intuitive job search assistance, streamlined  job descriptions, forward-looking career planning, and bespoke career support. Further, we integrate additional technologies, including video and content search AP Is, to enrich the app's resource offerings with vis ual and informative content. Our dedication to simplifying language and improving the user experience ensures that our users can confidently make well-informed decisions about their futures.  We have built a substantial database, largely sourced from the O*NET OnLine database sponsored by the US Department of Labor. This database, the cornerstone of our application, provides users with  detailed job descriptions, educational requirement s, and potential earnings. As our app engages more users, our algorithms dynamically integrate AI-generated responses into the database, keeping it up-to-date and responsive to user interaction. Achievify and learning We have considered the intersections of diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) with computer-supported collaborative learning (CSCL), alongside the design  considerations for DEI. These efforts aim to foste r changes in education and create a nurturing and empowering environment (Borge, 2023; Gomez et al., 2021; Uttamchandani et al., 2020).  Achievify has been crafted with innovation to serve  individuals from underserved communities, prioritizing inclusivity and accessibi lity. Achievify helps to overcome challenges such as cultural misunderstandings, skill awareness deficits, and ob stacles to accessing employment information. Offeri ng unbiased career forecasts and educational materials, Achievify extends vocational possibilities beyond  the realm of formal education. It plays a pivotal role in bridging the educational and workforce gap for those in underserved communities. As an educational tool, Achievify enhances users' career awareness by introducing a wide array of professions and industry sectors (Figure 1), thus broadening their workforce perspectives. It supports the learning journey by aligning users' skills, interests, and p ersonal considerations with suitable career paths, and provides advice on skill enhancement for professional develo pment. Achievify has undergone five human-centered development cycles, concentrating on technological enhancements and user experience. These phases, steered by user insights, expert analysis, and our dedication to underserved communities, not only augment the platform's efficacy but also inculcate the ethos of perpetual learning and adaptability in its users. Figure 1 Achievify’s Platform Career Matching (left) and Predictive Roadmaps (right) Achievify provides predictive roadmaps that guide users through the necessary steps for various career paths in a clear and systematic way. This feature of the platf orm delivers a structured approach to career develo pment that is highly informative. Moreover, engaging with Achievify's digital interface serves as practical training in digital literacy, an increasingly essential skill in the mo dern workforce. Achievify stands as a holistic lear ning platform that equips individuals from underserved communities with the knowledge, skills, and mindset required to succeed in today's evolving job market, offering much more than mere career guidance. Achievify distinguishes itself by integrating diversity, equity, and inclusion with t he principles of computer-supported collaborative learning. It narrows the gap between education and the workforce  by acquainting users with a variety of professions, thus widening their career outlook. The platform's learn ing methodology is dynamic, seeking user feedback f or ongoing enhancement, and underlines the significance of resilience, adaptability, and digital literacy in the current employment landscape. Achievify not only encourages career exploration but also promotes a culture of continual learning, establishing itself as an all-encompassing instrument for both personal and professional growth.

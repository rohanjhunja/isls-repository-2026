# Designing Inclusive Storytelling Games: Leveraging Generative AI to Foster Neuromixed Collaboration

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
This work presents a design framework for neuromixed storytelling games that center inclusiveness, affirmation, safety, and equity. Grounded in neurodiversity and learning theories, the framework explores how Generative AI (GenAI) can support equitable participation and meaningful collaboration among neurodivergent and neurotypical players.

## Introduction

Serious games offer a promising avenue to support collaboration between neurotypical and neurodivergent players by creating inclusive digital spaces that account f or diverse needs and communication styles (Ringland, 2019; Carneiro et al., 2024). Yet, existing games often l ack mechanisms for neurodiverse interaction or oper ate under deficit-based models that frame neurodivergence as a problem t o fix (Dwyer, 2022). To shift this paradigm, we adopt a neurodiversity framework that embraces cogn itive differences as natural variations. In combina tion with GenAI, this approach enables inclusive storytelling  that affirms diverse strengths and fosters cross-neurotype collaboration.

This framework draws on five theoretical foundations in learning. Social constructivism (Vygotsky, 1978) emphasizes learning through collaboration and share d experiences, aligning with the co-creative nature of storytelling games. Universal Design for Learning (UDL) offers a flexible structure supporting diverse  sensory, cognitive, and communicative needs, enabling multim odal player interaction and choice (Cole  et al., 2024). Multimodal theory leverages various sensory and cognitive channels—visual, verbal, and interactive elements— to enhance inclusivity, understanding, and creative  expression (Piedade et al., 2023). Collaborative v isual storytelling (Zolyomi & Snyder, 2023) emphasizes teamwork and shared creati vity, making it ideal for neuromixed collaboration. Collectively, these theoretical perspectives underpin a design framework pro moting inclusive, affirming, safe, and equitable neuromixed digital play environments. Design GenAI-enabled inclusive storytelling games Adapting a neurodiversity approach  and evolving from previous work (Xiao, 2024), we in troduce the following principles as foundational parts of the inclusive design framework: Inclusiveness (Cezarotto, 2023; Sturm et al., 2018) ensures that all players, regardless of neurotype, can meaningfully participate by recognizing and valuing their unique contributions. Affirmation (Piedade, et al, 2023) celebrates neurodivergent id entities, creating an empowering environment where players can express themselves authentically. Safety (Rocheleau & Chiasson, 2022) is upheld through thoughtful mechanisms that minimize exclusion, misunderstanding, or harm, fostering trust and collaboration among players. Equity (Keil, 2024) emphasizes providing diverse pathways to success, ensuring that players with varying needs and preferences can thrive.

Integrating GenAI into the design framework While traditional storytelling games offer collaborative, narrative-rich experiences shaped by player choices (Sun et al., 2023), our framework advances these possibilities by harnessing GenAI tools—such as GPT-4, DALL-E, and ComfyUI —to enable more dynamic content generation, multimodal interaction, and role-based creativity. Players can co-create stories via prompts, visuals, and dialogue, allowing each player to contribute based on their unique strengths—such as visual thinking or narrative construction—while fostering inclusiveness and mutual respect.

Figure 1 illustrates a conceptual design loop where two players co-create a character by sketching animals. A generative image model (GIM) transforms their drawings into stylized images. Vision-language model (VLM) generates descriptions, which large language model (LLM) then uses to embed the characters into the game’s evolving narrative. These AI-driven elements support both structured storytelling and open-ended creativity, allowing players to rearrange story sequences or contribute new visual ideas.

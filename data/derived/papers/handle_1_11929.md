# Generative AI in Norwegian Classrooms: Differences in Teaching About, With and Through GAI

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
The use of chatbots in classroom teaching presents both challenges and opportunities for students' learning. From a CSCL standpoint, our analysis reveals that many students encounter difficulties in their communication with adapted chatbots, even though the chatbots were designed to support students' work with domain knowledge, in their learning activities. This indicates that teachers need to devote more time and effort to support students' learning processes by balancing the tensions between learning about generative AI and learning with generative AI in various domains. Teachers must carefully consider, select, and remind students about specific lesson aims and relevant content.

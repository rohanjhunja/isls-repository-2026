# Knowledge Mapping and Trend Analysis of AI Agents in Education: A Bibliometric Study from 2004-2024

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence technologies is reshaping the paradigm of educational technology applications. Against this backdrop, the application of AI agents in the field of education has emerged as a cutting-edge topic in academia. However, existing studies lack a systematic theoretical framework. To address this research gap, this study employs bibliometric methods to analyze relevant literature from the Scopus database spanning 2004 to 2024, constructing a comprehensive knowledge map of AI agent research in education. Through multidimensional analytical approaches, including co-occurrence analysis, clustering analysis, and burst analysis, the study systematically examines the developmental trajectory and evolutionary pathways of AI agents in education. Overall, this research presents a clear framework of existing studies on AI agents in education, offering deep insights into the intelligent transformation of educational technology.

## Introduction

With the rapid development of artificial intelligen ce technology, AI agents, as intelligent systems ca pable of autonomous decision-making and taking actions to achieve specific goals, are revolutionizing the education field (Wang et al., 2025). The emergence of large languag e models represented by ChatGPT has fundamentally changed how technology is used in traditional educa tional environments (Wang et al., 2024a). Research shows that AI agents have enormous potential in constructing learning environments and optimizing instructional design. This includes providing real-time feedback, establishing intelligent virtual lea rning environments, and assisting teachers in curriculum development (Wang et al., 2025). Identifying research gaps by examining relevant lit erature helps to further refine the theoretical foundation of research topics. However, existing li terature reviews mostly explore these issues from a technological application perspective, such as the studies by Razak et al. (2023) and Rakić et al. (2023), while systematic analysis remains insufficient.

Bibliometrics, as a precise quantitative research method, can objectively reveal the evolutionary path and research hotspots in a field. Therefore, this study adopts a bibliometric approach to systematically analyze research literature on AI agents in educ ation published in the Scopus database from 2004 to  2024, aiming to answer the following research questions: RQ1: How has the distribution Power of AI agents in education evolved over the past two decades? RQ2: What are the current key research trends and hotspots regarding AI agents in education?

## Research method

Bibliometrics is a scientific method based on quant itative analysis of literature data (Wang et al., 2 024b), which uses bibliometric software for visualization analys is and can systematically reveal research hotspots and development trends in research fiel ds. This method not only objectively presents the h istorical development trajectory of a discipline but also effectively pre dicts future research directions (Shang et al., 202 4; Wang et al., 2022). This study uses the VOSviewer analysis tool to conduct key word co-occurrence analysis and thematic evolution analysis of AI agent-related research in the education field from 2004 t o 2024, aiming to comprehensively reveal the development dynamics in this field. Data source The research selected the Scopus database as the so urce of literature, which has extensive disciplinar y coverage and high-quality literature inclusion. The specific search q uery was: TITLE-ABS-KEY (("AI-Agent*" OR "Intelligent agent") AND ("instruct*" OR "teach*" OR "education")). The search time range w as from January 1, 2004, to November 8, 2024, limited to English li terature. The initial search yielded 2,363 records,  and after screening (see Figure 1), 462 core papers were finally identified as the analysis sample. Figure 1 PRISMA Screening Flowchart

## Bibliometric results and analysis

Distribution power Literature descriptive statistical analysis This study first presents the descriptive statistic al characteristics of AI agent literature in the fi eld of education. These publications were authored by 1,412 researche rs and published in 317 different academic journals. As of the retrieval cutoff date, these articles had been cited a total of 3,650 times. The research includes  1,113 author keywords and 2,889 extended keywords. Among the col lected literature, the h-index, which comprehensively measures the quantity and quality of academic output, reached 26. Trends in publications and disciplinary distribution From the annual publication volume of AI agent research in the field of education (Figure 2), it is evident that the overall research trend is on the rise. Between 2004  and 2008, publication volume steadily increased, r eaching its first peak in 2008, associated with the research bo om in Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS). From 2008  to 2016, the volume fluctuated and declined, but since 2017, with the development of large language models, research has entered a new growth phase. Following the emergence  of ChatGPT in 2022, studies on AI educational assi stants have accelerated significantly, reaching 57 publications in November 2024—4.38 times the volume of 2004. This rapid growth reflects that the application of AI in  education has entered a new stage. Further more, curve fitting suggests that as AI technology continues to advance  and educational demands deepen, research activity in this field will continue to grow.

Through the keyword co-occurrence clustering network (Figure 3a) and heatmap analysis (Figure 3b), we identified four core research clusters. Figure 3 Keyword clustering mapping (A) Co-occurrence clustering network (B) Keyword density Cluster 1-AI-Driven Innovation in Smart Educational Technologies Artificial intelligence, machine learning, and natural language processing technologies play a crucial role in the intelligent transformation of education. Thi s cluster focuses on how AI-driven technologies enhance teaching quality and optimize learning experiences,  emphasizing their integration with educational theo ries to achieve educational innovation. For example, machin e learning algorithms analyze learning data to prov ide personalized learning support (Romero & Ventura, 2020). Cluster 2-Building Intelligent Education Systems Based on Sma rt Agents. This cluster explores the application of software agents and virtual reality technology in intelligent educational systems, focu sing on the development of intelligent tutoring systems and the exploration of innovative teaching models. The integration of AI agent technology enables educational systems to pro vide personalized learning pathways and real-time feedback (Zhai et al., 2020). Additionally, the inc orporation of virtual reality  technology creates immersive experiences for learners, facilitating the understa nding of complex concepts (Kavanagh et al., 2017), demonstrating the potential of intelligent agents in reshaping education. Cluster 3-Knowledge-Driven Personalized Intelligent Education Practices. With the application of semantic web and knowledge representation technolog ies, personalized learning has become a significant  trend. This cluster investigates intelligent tutoring syst ems and personalized recommendation systems, explor ing how knowledge modeling meets the needs of different learners. Examples include personalized tutoring systems based on learner profiles (Mousavinasab et al., 2021) and  learning content recommendations enabled by semantic web technologies (Hu et al., 2022). Additionally, facto rs such as learning intentions and self-regulation capabilities have gained attention, providing insights for the development of future educational systems. Cluster 4-Exploring the Application of AI-Agent in Multi-Dimensional Educational Scenarios. AI agent represents an interdisciplinary integration of know ledge and plays a role in educational management informatization and decision support (Chen et al., 2020). The concept of "agents" incorporates theoretical methods from computer science, cognitive  science, and education (Quiroga et al., 2020). Inte lligent agents optimize the allocation of educational resources, promote interdisciplinary collaboration, drive the modernization of education, support effective decision-making, and enhance both educational quality and equity.

## Research evolutionary context

To gain a deeper understanding of the dynamic evolu tion of AI agents in the field of education, we ana lyzed the temporal distribution of keywords (Figure 4), showcasing the dynamic changes in research hotspots and trends in academic attention. From an overall developmental perspective, the research exhibits three distinct stages: Early Stage: The focus was on the interdisciplinary integration of foundational computer science theories with education, exploring the application of fundam ental technologies such as knowledge representation, multimedia systems, and intelligent agents in teach ing. This phase laid the technological foundation w hile also revealing the limitations of early technologies in educational practice. Figure 4 Time-Zone Evolution Map of Ketwords Mid Stage: Technology applications became more dive rsified, with the maturation of virtual reality, multi-agent systems, and online learning platforms. The r esearch focus shifted toward building inclusive and adaptive learning environments. The introduction of  machine learning and data mining pioneered a data-driven educational analytics paradigm, providing a scienti fic basis for decision-making (Nieto et al., 2023). This stage not only expanded the boundaries of technology applications but also deepened the understanding of technologyenabled education. Recent Stage: The rapid iteration of advanced techn ologies such as deep learning and natural language processing marks the deep integration of AI technologies, ushering education into a new era of intelligence. These technologies have demonstrated significant advantages in personalized learning and intelligent assessment (Owan et al., 2023) and are reshaping traditional teaching models and learning paradigms.

## Conclusion

This study systematically reviews research on AI-Agent in the field of education from 2004 to 2024 f rom a bibliometric perspective. Regarding the research pu blication power (RQ1), the analysis reveals a clear  phased characteristic in this field, which can be divided into three key periods: the initial expl oration period from 2004 to 2008, where research progressed steadily but slo wly, with an average annual publication volume rema ining low; the consolidation and adjustment period from 2008 to 2016, during which publication volume fluctuated but core research institutions and scholar groups gradu ally formed, laying a crucial foundation for subseq uent development; and the flourishing development period  from 2017 to the present, particularly in 2023-2024, when the number of publications saw explosive growth, wi th a significant increase in the number of institut ions and authors, marking the onset of an innovative and active phase in this research field. Regarding the research hotspots and trends of AI-Agent in education (RQ2), through keyword co-occurrence network analysis, we identified four mai n research theme clusters: AI-driven intelligent educational technology innovation, the construction of smart ed ucational systems based on intelligent agents, know ledgedriven personalized intelligent educational practic es, and the exploration of AI-Agent applications in multidimensional educational scenarios. The researc h trend indicates that the field is evolving  from early applications of basic technologies to the deep inte gration of advanced AI technologies such as deep le arning and natural language processing in education. Notably, the role of AI-Agent in education has evolved from a mere auxiliary tool to a core driving force for educatio nal innovation, signaling that educational intellig ence is accelerating towards a new phase.

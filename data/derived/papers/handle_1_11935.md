# Measuring the Effect of GenAI Chatbots in CSCL environments: A Study with PyramidApp

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
The present study explored the impact of a Generative AI (GenAI) chatbot in the PyramidApp (CSCL) tool), using a within-subjects design. Results showed the chatbot improved student answer accuracy and positively influenced both individual and group interactions. Also suggest that GenAI chatbots are valued in the educational sphere by enhancing learning outcomes and collaboration in digital education environments.

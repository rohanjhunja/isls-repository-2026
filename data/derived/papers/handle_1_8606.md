# Informing Advanced Digital Technologies’ Design for Refugee Language Education in Greece

**Conference:** ISLS 2022

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
This paper focuses on needs finding for computer-assisted language learning for vulnerable population living in difficult circumstances. It presents semi-structured interviews with teachers and volunteers working with refugee students in Greece. It explores their needs regarding digital technologies in teaching and learning of Modern Greek as a second language. The findings outline the context of technology and Greek refugee language education, the capacity that teachers have, as well as the challenges within this context.

## Context

This paper aims to explore the ways that advanced digital technologies, such as mobile devices and applications, and artificial intelligence tools, can enhance seco nd language learning and instruction within the con text of refugees in Greece. Refugee language education in G reece is mostly supported by non-governmental organizations, community-based organizations of vol unteers and some training language programs that we re designed by Ministries, Municipalities and Universities (Kantzou et. al, 2017). The use of digital technologies in such a context appears to have promising results, according to McCaffrey & Taha (2019) who studied the use of smartphones by recent refugees in the US with the purpose to adapt and make sense of the world moving across places and named languages.

Objectives The research questions associated with this small-scale study include the following: ¥ What are the needs of refugee language teachers within the Greek context? ¥ What technology design features would better support refugee language education in Greece? ¥ What are the potential challenges for teachers working with advanced digital technologies?

## Methods and data

This paper follows a qualitative case study methodology. Four semi-structured interviews of approximately 1 hour each were conducted with teachers and volunteers of  elementary or middle school educational background, working in informal settings with refugee students in Greece and having an average of 4.25 years of experience in the field. Teachers were identified through snowball sampling during the Fall of 2019. The interview included questions about: Language course structure and material, Use and access of digital technology tools in educational practice, Use and access to digital technologies cr afted for language education. The interviews were a nalyzed following thematic coding analysis. Member checking was realized for validity purposes. ## Results

Findings illustrate the current context of technology use in the Greek refugee language education, the needs and the capacity that teachers have, the applicability of advanced digital technologies, and its potential  challenges. The findings show that websites, mobile applications, or platforms are used and are considered helpful to achieve better communication. Participants highlighted that  digital tools’ design should aim in improving ever yday meaning making and being accessible in the native language of the users. Teachers reported minimal or no training in incorporating digital tools in their instruction  and subsequently, they lack proper information abo ut the availability of digital instructional tools for this specific context. About the potential challenges, they mentioned: digitally illiterate students, lack of a common language for communication, high instability and refugee population movement, high cost of the AI-enhanced tools, challenging weather and infrastructure conditions.

## Discussion & scholarly significance

Advanced digital technologies seem promising, benef icial and are viewed positively. However, there are  more fundamental needs to be addressed alongside the pot ential of using innovative digital tools, such as t he environmental challenges and the inadequate teacher  training. Overall, this paper addresses some desig n considerations for devices, platforms, and applications of second language learning in complex settings.

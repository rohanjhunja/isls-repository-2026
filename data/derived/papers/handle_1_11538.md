# Evaluating Efficacy of Problematizing Chatbot in Higher Education

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract

Five graduate students brainstormed with a problematizing chatbot for project work. Reflections showed that despite some discomfort with persistent questioning, the brainstorming experience was rich and productive overall. Grading student chats showed high engagement but inadequate use of prompting strategies and weak critical analyses of AI-generated responses. Results implicate a greater focus on AI literacy training that facilitates knowledge of effective prompting and awareness of the limitations of generative AI to encourage critiquing its output.

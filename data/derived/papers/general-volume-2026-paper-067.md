By creating a virtual space for ISLS participants, the Dashboard will be utilized as a collaborative tool as well as a demo, allowing participants to find common interests, affinities and, potentially, even to foster continuing connections. Participants’ Dashboards will be displayed in multiple modalities – they will be welcomed to edit their Dashboard profile or view others’ Dashboards on their own web-capable device or at our station, where Dashboards will be displayed ambiently. After the initial demo session, we will keep a version of the Dashboard running for participants who are interested in exploring a representation of ISLS attendees’ affinities, and as a low-stakes networking tool.

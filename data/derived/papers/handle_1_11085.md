## Background

Given the shortage of high school counselors (American Sc hool Counselor Association, 2023) and the difficulty of navigating the Free Application for Federal Stud ent Aid (FAFSA) form (Dynarski & Scott-Clayton, 2013), high school students-especially those from disadvantaged backgrounds-turn to free online sources for help with college application and financial aid (Brown et al., 2016). One such resource is the subreddit r/ApplyingtoCollege on the free website Reddit, with a detailed FAQ and over one million subscribers (Social Rise, 2023). Recent developments in generative AI have made chatbots popular sources of information; ChatGPT, a well-known chatbot interface of OpenAI’s GPT large language model, is used by 42% of students (Walton Family Foundation, 2023). In light of Doroudi’s (2023) recent call for the Le arning Sciences to revisit AI as a tool for learning, we wondered if an untrained ChatGPT based on GPT-3.5 (from here on referred to only as "ChatGPT"), which is also free to the public, could advise students regarding financial aid and the FAFSA and how it would compare to advice given by humans in the r/ApplyingtoCollege FAQs. The authors will then discuss suggestions for practice and implications for learning and curriculum.

## Methodology

The researchers were two master's students in a lea rning sciences program at a private university in t he northeastern United States. Both had advised studen ts on applying to U.S. colleges. The authors took t he list of seven questions in the financial aid section from r /ApplyingtoCollege's FAQs on Reddit (freeport_aidan, 2023), combined questions 5 and 6 together and put the six questions into a newly registered ChatGPT account to avoid an old account's memories from past conversations. The questions can be viewed at https://tinyurl.com/bjauh5ac. Due to the novelty of AI-based answer evaluation frameworks, we developed a new that assesses answers numerically via seven variables, five concerning the content of the answers and two concerning the format. Each variable is rated at a scale of "0, 1, 2." The five  content variables of this framework are shown in F igure 1, and are based on conventional qualitative content analysis (Hs ieh & Shannon, 2005), which allowed us to compare and contrast the quality of answers despite limited existing literature. One author evaluated all Reddit answers and the oth er all ChatGPT answers. We used the code book definitions to resolve uncertainties or disagreements through discussion. ## Results

## Discussion

Our findings suggest an untrained ChatGPT has the potential to outperform meticulously human-written FAQs in advising about college financial aid application, e specially at providing clear task steps and easy-to-follow formatting. This implies counselors and counseling framework could potentially shift to developing stu dents' competency in prompt engineering to fully utilize AI chatbots. This, in turn, supports the importance of developing AI literacy in K-12 students, especially students without technical backgrounds (Long & Magerko, 2020). Finally, our evaluation framework could be further developed to assess college advising. The authors recognize the exploratory nature and limitations of their work. The authors used GPT-3.5 without the latest data compared to the GPT-4 (OpenAI, n.d.). We only used a limited list of questions from one subreddit, introducing potential bias. Future work could incorporate a wider range of questions and more diverse data sources.

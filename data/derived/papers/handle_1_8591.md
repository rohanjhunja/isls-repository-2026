# Supporting Multilingualism in Preschool: A Play Responsive Teaching Approach Mediated by Digital Technologies

**Conference:** ISLS 2022

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
This design-based research focuses on timely challenges such as multilingualism and the use of digital technologies that teaching in preschool is facing. Play-responsive teaching is the theory that informs the design and implementation of the study. Data will be generated from recordings where children and teachers engage in playful activities with tablets where multiple languages may be used. Analysis will investigate how teaching in preschool can be responsive to multilingualism by utilizing digital technologies (tablets).

## Introduction

In our globalized world, multiculturalism is the societal norm for many contemporary societies and according to Turner and Cross (2016, p.289) today’s education re flects an “increasing normalisation of multilingual ism”. However, even though multilingualism is now regarde d as a valuable asset, there is still a need to ide ntify pedagogical approaches, strategies and practical concepts for teaching in such circumstances. Purpose The purpose of this study is to produce new pedagogical knowledge oriented towards teaching in the field of Early Childhood Education (ECE) regarding multilingualism and digital technologies. More precisely, by apply ing a design-based research methodology it will be empirically explored how teaching in preschool can be responsive to a multilingual context, when playful activities with tablets (book creator app) are developed and teachers build pedagogy (teaching) on some principles of play-responsive teaching theory (PRECEC; Pramling et al., 2019). The research questions are:

- How do activities with digital technologies (tablet s) develop in linguistically diverse groups with

preschool teachers building pedagogy (teaching) on PRECEC?

- What are the challenges and opportunities preschool  teachers identify when reflecting on working

according to some principles of PRECEC, in linguistically diverse groups of children and using digital technologies?

## Method

Figure 1 A design-based research approach (from Reeves, 2006) ICLS2022 Proceedings 1822 © ISLS Design-based Research (Brown, 1992; Collins, 1992) is chosen because it corresponds to the dual focus of this study to develop theory (PRECEC) and to improve pra ctice (teaching in preschool). Design-based Researc h (DBR) is characterized as theoretically oriented, interventionist, iterative, and collaborative (McKenney & Reeves, 2018). Those characteristics will be included in th is study and are viewed as standards/commitments fo r the quality of DBR (see Figure1).

Two preschool classrooms from two different interna tional preschools in Sweden will participate in thi s study.

The study will consists of observations of preschool environments, field notes, interviews with the principals and preschool teachers, and video recordings of playful  activities. Since the study involves video recordi ngs where children participate, it has been ethically approved by the Swedish Ethical Review Authority.

## Expected Findings

Findings will clarify:

- How teachers work with some principles of PRECEC to engage multilingual children in playful activities

with digital technologies.

- What kind of interactions develop between teacher-children or between peers during these activities.

- An understanding of how the mediation of digital technologies in the playful activities might change or

expand the nature of PRECEC.

- Challenges and opportunities that preschool teachers identify after participating in this study.

## Conclusion

This study, addressing the issues of multilingualism and the use of digital technologies in preschool, is timely and will make important contributions, both to the exis ting body of knowledge about and in ECE and to a wi der societal discussion on these matters. Generalizability is established by placing the findings of this study in a broad theoretical context (PRECEC) to demonstrate whether and how the study is an example of the phenomenon under investigation.

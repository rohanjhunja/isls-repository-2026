# “Computer Science Is About Rethinking and Reshaping Reality”: A Social Studies Teacher’s Transformation Through the Co-Designing of Computer Science-Infused Lessons

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract

Integrating computer science (CS) concepts and practices into K-12 education mainly emphasizes science, technology, engineering, and mathematics subjects, more than social studies. We examine the transformation of a middle school geography teacher’s perspective as the teacher incorporates CS content into their teaching through a co-design process. Utilizing the Interconnected Model of Teacher Professional Growth, we analyze interviews and classroom observations to describe shifts in the teacher’s practices, beliefs, and understanding of CS integration. Our findings suggest that the teacher redefined CS from a set of technical skills to a human-centered discipline, making geography learning more engaging. Additionally, the co-design allowed the teacher to view students as active creators of digital artifacts and to embrace their own role as a creator able to modify existing technologies to better suit their educational context. Our findings indicate promising ways to engage teachers from non-STEM backgrounds in effectively connecting their subjects with CS.

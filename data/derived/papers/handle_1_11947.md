# Investigating Novice Teachers’ Practices in the Value of Collaborative Learning Processes through Knowledge Building Principles and a CSCL Environment

**Conference:** ISLS 2025

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
This study presents how novice teachers approach knowledge building (KB) principles and how they perceived their first KB teaching in the classroom, and a CSCL environment called Knowledge Forum (KF) to facilitate collaborative learning. Four novice teachers in northern Taiwan were recruited to share how they perceived and reflected through in-depth interviews. Qualitative analysis showed novice teachers begin to develop KB strategies to value collaborative learning in KB principles and identify ideas evaluation in KF environments.

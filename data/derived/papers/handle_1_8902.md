# LF-LKT: A Logistic Regression Knowledge Tracing Model Integrating Learning and Forgetting

**Conference:** ISLS 2022

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
In the process of learning, learning behavior and forgetting behavior are interwoven, and students' forgetting behavior has great influence on Knowledge Tracing (KT). In order to accurately model learning and forgetting behaviors, this paper proposes a learning-forgetting logistic knowledge tracing (LF-LKT) model that integrated students’ forgetting factors. Three factors affecting knowledge forgetting, including the time interval of KC, KC presentation sequence (that is, the recency effect), learning opportunities of KC, and students’ response of KC are considered in this paper. We also give different level of weights to time interval. The proposed model is compared with other four models on three dataset. Results shows LF-LKT improves the predictive performance as compared to AFM, PFA. Moreover, the ablation study was conducted to investigate the influence of different factors and the result suggest the combination of three factors results in performance improvement.

## Introduction

As the "brain" of the adaptive learning system, the learner model undertakes the important responsibility of selfadaptation. Specifically, in the process of learners' learning, the system relies on learners' historical learning data to construct learners' models. Based on structured subject knowledge and diversified learning resources, adaptive learning system provides students with the most sui table learning resources, paths and modes by collec ting interactive data of students' learning process and utilizing technologies such as cognitive diagnosis,  machine learning and personalized recommendation. The system can also feedback the knowledge mastery level of learners for educators, so that educators can   identify the  needs of learners, so as to better adjust teaching  strategies. Therefore, learner modeling plays an important role  in realizing personalized education. How to build a better learner model, how to combine the learner model wit h the design of teaching environment, and how to us e the learner model in the learning environment to achiev e personalized education are the core technical pro blems in the next stage.

Knowledge Tracing, builds the knowledge state model of learners through accurately modeling students’ mastery level of a knowledge component over time based on their historical learning trajectories. KT is the earliest and most concerned learner modeling technology, which can provide learners with adaptive teaching support and push personalized learning resources, so as to improve learning effect and efficiency (Wang,2013).The knowledge tracing model is based on the interactive data of l earners and the learning system. The theory of educ ational psychology and the theory of Ebbinghaus forgetting curve(Ebbinghaus,2013; Murre,2015) put forward that students forget what they have learned, and the impact of forgetting is the decline of knowledge mastery. Recent studies have considered students' forgetting behavi or. However, how to adopt classical theories in tra ditional education, such as forgetting curve to predict know ledge state, is still in its infancy. Moreover, the re are few studies considering recency effect. Inspired by the  recency effect of psychological theory, this paper  integrates factors such as the presentation of knowledge component into the model to predict students' knowledge state. Proposed model LF-LKT is based on Logistic Regression (LR) model(K leinbaum,2002), which uses one or more continuous o r discrete independent variables to analyze and predict discrete dependent variables. Logistic regression is one of the common methods of data mining and machine learning. ## Discussion

In this paper, the time interval of KC, KC presentation sequence (that is, the recency effect), learning opportunities of KC, and students’ response of KC are considered.  A knowledge tracing model LF-LKT based on logistic regression model is proposed, which takes into account both learning and forgetting factors. The result shows that LF-LKT model improves the predictive performance as compared to traditional knowledge tracing models based on logistic regression model, such as AFM,PFA,R-PFA  and PFA-decay models. Moreover, the ablation study was conducted to investigate the influence of diffe rent factors and the result suggest the combination  of three factors results in performance improvement.

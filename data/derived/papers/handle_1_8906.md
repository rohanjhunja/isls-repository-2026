# Study on Evaluation Indicator System of Interdisciplinary Problem-Solving Ability for Pre-Service STEM Teachers

**Conference:** ISLS 2022

## Abstract & Introduction

### Abstract
With the advent of the era of artificial intelligence, the problems that students need to solve are more diversified and integrated. Therefore, it is very necessary to cultivate interdisciplinary problem-solving (IPS) ability for students. Pre-service STEM teachers play a very important role in interdisciplinary education, the analysis and evaluation of IPS ability of pre-service STEM teachers is helpful to understand the current ability level. Based on the literature review and the Delphi method, an evaluation indicator system for pre-service STEM teachers' IPS ability is constructed. Expert consultation and Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) method are used to determine the weight of indicators. 98 surveys from 10 colleges are collected, results indicated that the evaluation indicator system has high validity, pre-service STEM teachers' IPS ability is generally average. Finally, directions are also provided for evaluating and improving the IPS ability of pre-service STEM teachers.

## Introduction

With the constant development of innovative technol ogy, the problems that students need to solve are m ore diversified and integrated, the social demand for student is becoming more comprehensive. Cultivating learners’ interdisciplinary problem-solving (IPS) ability have gradually become one of the key training objectiv es in the educational reform of most countries. In order to improve IPS ability of pre-service STEM teachers, the Australian and UK governments have issued corresponding polici es towards providing high-quality special training and learning support. The Chinese government has also t aken measures to construct specialist teacher group s with outstanding IPS ability through multi-school collaborative interdisciplinary teaching and research act ivities (Ministry of Education, 2019). IPS ability is impor tant to students, however, pre-service STEM teacher s as the ones who develop this ability in students, must als o possess IPS ability (Ming & Sha, 2019). Researche rs have evaluated middle school students’ IPS ability (Feng, 2019). However, there are little research on teac hers’ IPS ability. Existing research mainly focus on the orie ntation of STEM instruction (Günbatar & Bakırcı, 20 19), improving pre-service teachers’ STEM knowledge and problem-solving skills ((Berisha & Vula，2021; Özçakır Sümen & Çalışıcı, 2021). It has been an urgent need  to cultivate IPS ability of pre-service STEM teach ers. Previously, the concept of pre-service STEM teacher s' IPS ability is needed to be clarified. Secondly,  a corresponding indicator framework is necessary to e stimate the ability. Therefore, in order to underst and the current situation of IPS ability of pre-service tea chers, it is necessary to establish the correspondi ng indicator framework that candiagnose pre-service teachers’ IPS ability level.

## Theoretical framework

IPS ability of pre-service STEM teachers is mainly discussed in this article. STEM teacher refers to t eachers of subjects related to STEM such as mathematics, science and information technology. Pre-service teachers majoring in Educational Technology are good at STEM education and science education (Ming & Sha, 2019). IPS ability means that when pre-service STEM teachers encounter  interdisciplinary problems in teaching, based on t he attitude of facing the problem, combine the methods and strategies of multidisciplinary knowledge when dealing with problems, as well as the quality of interdisci plinary problem solving in exploring and integratin g different disciplines.

Construction of evaluation indicator system The cultivation of IPS ability is the trend of the times and one of the goals of current education. In this study, we refer to the outline of STEM teacher ability indicator system issued by China Institute of Education Sciences. The dimensions of problem-solving are referred to relevant literature as well (Huang Maozai & Chen Wendian, 2004). Then, an evaluation indicator system is constructed  initially to evaluate the IPS ability of pre-servi ce STEM teacher combining with the characteristics of pre-service teachers. Expert consultation questionnaire is designed ICLS2022 Proceedings 965 © ISLS to further determine evaluation indicator system. F inally, pre-service STEM teacher’ IPS ability evalu ation indicator system includes 3 first-level indicators and 9 secondary indicators (see Figure 1). Figure 1 Evaluation Indicator System of Pre-service STEM Teachers' IPS Ability The attitude dimension of interdisciplinary problem solving can be divided into three aspects of cogni tion, emotion and intention. “Attitude" refers to individual's stable psychological tendency and response to  others or things, including cognition, emotion, and intention, which are usually manifested in beliefs, feelings, and behavioral tendencies (Shi, 2012). "Cognition" refe rs to the understanding and problem-solving beliefs  of preservice STEM teachers. "Emotion" is an emotional experience from the inside. "Intention" is the behavior of the pre-service STEM teacher’s sense of responsibility and mission to solve interdisciplinary problems, wh ich is manifested by cognitive and emotional factors.

Interdisciplinary problem-solving methods and strat egies dimension refers to the use of interdisciplin ary thoughts and knowledge to solve problems. In this study, methods and strategies are divided into three parts (Su, 2017). The understanding and conception part indicates pre-service STEM teachers' understanding of knowledge involved in interdisciplinary problems and their co nception of knowledge performance. The development and integration part indicates pre-service STEM teachers’ competence to obtain materials through multiple channels when developing teaching resources, integrate resources from the perspective of cultivating students' abilities in various aspects. The implementation and evaluation part refers to the methods and strategies used by pre-service STEM teachers to solve interdisciplinary problems in teaching organization and teaching implementation, it also can evaluate and optimize the process of solving interdisciplinary problems. The quality dimension of interdisciplinary problem solving refers to the active learning, innovative performance and communication and collaboration abi lities of pre-service STEM teachers in the process of solving interdisciplinary problems (Huang Maozai & Chen Wendian, 2004). Firstly, pre-service STEM teachers can recognize interdisciplinary problems, use appropriate methods to solve problems, improve the solutions and promote professional development. Secondly, pre-service STEM teachers need to show a unique way of thinking at all times, discover problems creatively. Finally, the development of pre-service STEM teachers emph asizes teamwork spirit, which stand for a harmonious atmosphere to share resources, communicate with each other, and jointly complete solutions to problems in cooperation.

## Research questions

Based on the above construction of the evaluation i ndicator system, the current study mainly focuses o n two questions:

(a) How to verify the effectiveness of the pre-serv ice STEM teacher’s IPS ability evaluation indicator  system?

(b)What level do the study objects perform in each dimension of IPS ability?

## Research Design

Determine indicator weight In the study, the expert consultation questionnaire of the indicator weight is designed after the construction of the evaluation indicator system. Then, the average (Lik ert five-point scale) is used to express the degree  of the importance of each evaluation indicator, and the co ncentration of expert opinions is expressed by medi an and majority. Experts' opinions on each indicator are collected to delete indicators with low support. After two rounds of consultation by 12 experts, the evaluation indicator system is also determined. The Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) is a decision-making method and proposed by the American operations researcher Saatty T.L. Firstly, an analysis structure model of the pre-service STEM teacher's IPS ability ICLS2022 Proceedings 966 © ISLS evaluation indicator system is established in the yaahp software. Secondly, the 1-9 ratio scale method is introduced to compare and analyze the factors of each layer in pairs, distinguishing which element is more important. Finally, the judgment matrix is constructed. The weight of t he pre-service STEM teacher's IPS ability rating in dicator system is established through calculation and analysis of the experts' scoring of the importance of each indicator in the judgment matrix at the last (see Table 1).

## Participants

The survey objects cover senior undergraduate in educational technology and postgraduate in modern education technology from 10 normal colleges in China. The pa rticipants have mastered professional knowledge and teaching skills, and have participated in educational internships for 3-6 months in primary and secondary schools. However, they haven’t entered the teaching field formally, lacking educational practical experience.

## Procedure

A total of 104 questionnaires are collected in this survey and 98 are valid questionnaires, with an effective rate of 94.23%. Through SPSS analysis of the overall validity of the questionnaire, it is concluded that the overall KMO coefficient of the questionnaire survey is 0.885, t he overall Alpha reliability coefficient is 0.954 a nd the Alpha reliability coefficient of each dimension type is g reater than 0.7, which proves that the reliability of the questionnaire is relatively high.

## Results

RQ1: How to verify the effectiveness of the pre-service STEM teacher’s IPS ability evaluation indicator system?

Firstly, the pre-service STEM teacher’s IPS ability evaluation indicator system is based on literature analysis and the Delphi method, more importantly, it is determin ed after two rounds of expert consultation. Secondl y, the indicators weights at all levels are determined acc ording to the analytic hierarchy process (AHP). Thi rdly, the questionnaire is developed by using the pre-service  STEM teacher's IPS ability evaluation indicator sy stem. Finally, the result shows that the IPS ability levels of the research objects are normally distributed, the overall IPS ability is average level. In addition, some research objects claimed that the allocation of the evaluation indicator system is relatively reasonable, reflecting the req uirements for specific abilities such as subject kn owledge and operational skills at each stage of IPS. Therefore,  the IPS ability evaluation indicator system design ed in this research has high scientific and use-value.

## Conclusion

The evaluation of pre-service STEM teachers' IPS ability is the prerequisite for STEM education. The localized design and development of the evaluation indicator system is a necessary way to evaluate the training effect of pre-service STEM teachers. In this study, the Delphi method is used to construct an evaluation indicator system for pre-service STEM teachers' IPS ability, the ana lytic hierarchy process (AHP) is also used to calcu late the weights of indicators at all levels. The practical result shows that the evaluation indicator system candiagnose the pre-service STEM teachers' IPS ability levels and existing shortcomings to some extent. However, it’s a complex system engineering to construct a scientific and reliable localized evaluation indicator system, which needs to be continuously revised and improved based on the evaluation object and the educational environment. In the future, the evaluation indicator system should be expanded in such aspects as the survey scope of regions and grades.
